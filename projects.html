A Reinforcement Learning Approach to Active Learning.

It is an ongoing research project mentored by Yue Wang. We are trying to apply a Reinforcement Learning Approach to Active Learning.

Reproduce a 384-bit RSA Attack.

It is a course challenge in VE475 Introduction to Cryptography, 2017 Summer. We are asked to produce the private key given the 384-bit RSA public key. In 2015, the research paper Factoring as a Service states that it is not diffcult to factor 512-bit RSA public keys. I reproduced such an attack on Amazon EC2 and winned the challenge.

Robocup Small Size Soccer League.

RoboCup is an annual international robotics competition, and the competition is between two groups whose robots play soccer. The key feature is that group member cannot control robots manually and the competition is completely automatic. Our team SRC won the world championship in 2017.

Mathematical Contest in Modeling (2017).

In the MCM, we are asked to analyze the effect of allowing self-driving cars in the courtry on the traffic of the whole city. Given few data, we use Gravity Potential Model to generalize the traffic flow and then apply Flow Network Model to assess the effect quantatively. The left image is one of our predictions. We got Honorable Mention finally.

Intelligent Venue Check-in System.

This is our product implemented in HackxFDU, one of the top hackathons in China. Powered by Microsoft Cognitive Service and Kinect, it can record the distribution and emotion of people when check-in. We got Microsoft's reward finally. Our code is open source at Github.
