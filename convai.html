Abstract

We aim to bring vision to the blind or people with visual problems through conversational platform. By question answering and dialogue between users and the machine, users could gain sufficient information from the image(s) and scene(s). To achieve this goal, our product supports two main functions: (1) Fine-grained people detection (2) Coarse-grained image caption. Users could gain different aspects of people in an image: number of people, their gender, their age and their emotion. For low vision community, getting these low-level detail could help them understand the world better. Users could also gain descriptive information from the image through the second function. The product provides coarse-grained scene understanding caption through online image to speech transformation.
