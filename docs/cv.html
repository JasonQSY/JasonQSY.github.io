Mathematical Contest in Modeling (2017). Jan. 2017

Team leader. Responsible for modeling.

Our objective is to analysis the effect of allowing self-driving cars in the courtry on the traffic of the whole city. Given few data, we use Gravity Potential Model to generalize the traffic flow and then apply Flow Network Model to assess the effect quantatively. In the generalization, we use cross-validation method and gradient descent optimization. Finally, we got Honorable Mention.
